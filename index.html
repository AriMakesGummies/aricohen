What I do

I build and optimize gelatin and pectin systems for gummies and soft chews. I focus on every aspect of gummy construction, including flavour, acid, buffering, masking, pH, water activity, appearance, shelf appeal, and stability.

  • High-load gummies: creatine, collagen and other heavy actives
  • Cannabis edibles: cannabinoid selection, ratios and input types
  • Formulation development and optimization
  • Hydrocolloid selection: gelatin, pectin, agar
  • Flavour development, acid and buffering systems
  • Reformulations for natural colours and flavours
  • Process troubleshooting and quality improvement
  • Ingredient and vendor sourcing
  • Room setup: equipment, ingredients, flavours
  • Training, in person or remote
  • Gummy formulations for sale
